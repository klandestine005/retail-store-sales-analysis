# 🛒 Retail Store Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data from 2022 to 2024 to identify sales trends, product performance, and customer purchasing behavior. The analysis provides actionable insights to support business decision-making.

## Objectives
- Analyze sales trends over time.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Provide recommendations to improve sales performance.

## Tools
- Python (Pandas)
- Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables & Dashboard)

## Dataset
- Retail Store Sales Dataset (Kaggle)

## Data Preparation
- Removed unnecessary columns.
- Handled missing values using mean and median imputation.
- Filled missing values in the Discount Applied column with **Unknown**.
- Filtered transactions to include only data from 2022–2024.

## Key Insights
- Sales showed seasonal patterns, with peaks at the beginning of each year and declines during October–November.
- Furniture demonstrated the most consistent sales growth, while Butchers and Milk Products showed declining trends.
- Online sales slightly outperformed in-store sales, highlighting the importance of digital channels.
- A strong positive correlation (r = 0.801) was found between purchase frequency and customer spending.

## Recommendations
- Launch promotional campaigns during low-sales periods.
- Prioritize inventory and marketing for high-performing product categories.
- Continue investing in digital marketing to strengthen online sales.
- Implement loyalty programs to increase customer retention and lifetime value.
